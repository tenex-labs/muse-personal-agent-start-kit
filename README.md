# Muse Personal Agent Starter Kit

**Give Muse one useful job. Teach it your preferences. Make it repeat.**

A practical guide from JJ Englert and Tenex for everyday life. No coding or hardware required.

## Start here

1. [Set up memory, SOUL, connections, and Feed](guides/setup.md).
2. Pick one of the ten workflows below. Replace the bracketed details and paste its prompt into Muse.
3. Review the result and give one specific correction.
4. [Make the useful version recurring](guides/routines.md).

**[Open the browser guide](https://tenex-labs.github.io/muse-personal-agent-start-kit-safe/)** for playable demos and copy buttons. You can also download the repository with **Code → Download ZIP**, unzip it, and open `docs/index.html`. Internet is required for the videos; the written guide works offline. GitHub’s README does not play HTML video embeds.

## Pick your first job

1. [Personal wardrobe picker](workflows/wardrobe.md) — Choose an outfit from clothes you actually own.
2. [Movie & TV picker](workflows/movies.md) — Find something you will actually want to watch.
3. [Food & restaurant finder](workflows/food.md) — Solve “what should we eat?” with choices that fit.
4. [Health & habit tracker](workflows/health.md) — Keep track of a habit you chose.
5. [Calendar watcher](workflows/calendar.md) — Catch conflicts and preparation needs early.
6. [Birthday reminders & gift ideas](workflows/birthdays.md) — Remember the people you choose and find thoughtful gifts.
7. [Weekend planner](workflows/weekend.md) — Find something worth leaving the house for.
8. [Car & purchase watcher](workflows/shopping.md) — Watch for a specific item without checking every day.
9. [School forms & family admin](workflows/forms.md) — Prepare the paperwork without retyping everything.
10. [Travel & packing helper](workflows/travel.md) — Get ready for a trip using the plans you already have.

Start with the **movie picker** or **weekend planner** if you want a simple first win.

# Give your setup a checkup

Use this after a few tasks, or when results drift.

```text
Review our setup for this job. Show the preferences you are using, the connected sources you can actually access, the latest artifact, and any active routine with its next run and timezone. Flag stale facts, conflicting instructions, duplicate routines, and missing access. Suggest the smallest useful fixes. Do not change settings or create tasks until I choose the fixes.
```

## Make the correction specific

| Instead of | Try |
| --- | --- |
| “These are bad recommendations.” | “Only movies under two hours. Exclude titles I rated down.” |
| “Stop bothering me.” | “Keep this job running, but alert only once per new qualifying result.” |
| “You forgot.” | “Show the saved preference for this job and update it to this rule.” |
| “Fix my calendar.” | “Show overlaps next week. Draft suggested changes for me to review.” |

Ask whether a change should apply to this result, this recurring job, or your general memory.

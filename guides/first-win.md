# Your first useful result

Start with a movie for tonight. No memory import, upload, or connected account is required.

1. Open Muse and start a side chat.
2. Paste this prompt.
3. Answer its questions.
4. Give one correction and compare the next result.

```text
Help me choose something to watch tonight. Ask me three short questions about my taste, streaming services, and available time. Then recommend three options, explain why each fits, and verify where I can watch them in my country. Label any availability you cannot check. Do not rent or buy anything.
```

Try: “Lighter tone. Under two hours. We already watched the second one.”

You should have three choices, reasons, and checked viewing options. Unverified availability should be labeled.

Ask it to turn the result into a watchlist artifact. Then [personalize your setup](setup.md), or [make the watchlist recurring](routines.md) after you like the result.

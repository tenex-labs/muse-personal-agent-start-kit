# Suggest an improvement

Open an issue with the step that failed, the expected result, and the Muse platform you used. Do not post personal memory, tokens, family records, or private screenshots.

For edits, keep the ten workflows practical and source product claims. Content lives in Markdown; keep matching browser-guide content in sync. Test links and copy buttons at phone and desktop widths. Use main as the pull request base.

## Visual style

Use the Muse style established by the video and thumbnail: deep navy and blue hero, cyan highlights, soft blue page background, white rounded cards, and simple blue line icons. Preserve the CSS tokens in `docs/index.html`. Do not replace it with the generic Tenex document theme. Check at desktop and phone widths before publishing.
